# Safeguarding and Child Protection Policy

**Effective Date: May 15, 2026**  
**Last Updated: May 15, 2026**

---

# 1. Introduction

The Presbyterian Church of Nigeria, First Abuja Parish ("PCN FAP", "the Church", "we", "our", or "us") is committed to maintaining a safe, respectful, secure, and Christ-centered environment for all persons interacting with our ministries, services, programs, events, and digital platforms.

This Safeguarding and Child Protection Policy establishes the standards, responsibilities, procedures, and protective measures implemented by PCN FAP to safeguard:

- Children and minors;
- Vulnerable adults;
- Members;
- Visitors;
- Volunteers;
- Workers;
- Ministers;
- Online participants;
- Individuals engaging through digital ministry platforms.

This policy applies to both physical and digital church environments.

---

# 2. Purpose of this Policy

This policy exists to:

- Protect children and vulnerable persons from harm;
- Establish safeguarding responsibilities;
- Prevent abuse, exploitation, neglect, harassment, and misconduct;
- Ensure responsible conduct by workers and volunteers;
- Promote safe online and offline ministry practices;
- Support lawful safeguarding escalation procedures;
- Comply with applicable safeguarding obligations and Nigerian laws;
- Support compliance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023.

---

# 3. Scope of Application

This policy applies to:

- Ministers;
- Pastors;
- Elders;
- Church workers;
- Employees;
- Volunteers;
- Youth leaders;
- Media teams;
- Technical teams;
- Prayer coordinators;
- Counselors;
- Vendors acting on behalf of the church;
- Digital platform administrators;
- All individuals participating in church activities.

It applies to:

- Physical church premises;
- Online worship services;
- Livestreams;
- WhatsApp groups;
- Telegram groups;
- Social media ministry;
- Online counseling;
- Video conferences;
- Church websites;
- Digital communication systems;
- Events and outreach programs.

---

# 4. Definitions

## 4.1 Child or Minor

A child or minor refers to any person under the age of 18 years.

## 4.2 Vulnerable Person

A vulnerable person includes individuals who may require additional protection due to:

- Age;
- Disability;
- Illness;
- Emotional distress;
- Dependency;
- Mental health concerns;
- Social vulnerability;
- Financial hardship;
- Crisis situations.

## 4.3 Safeguarding

Safeguarding refers to measures taken to protect individuals from:

- Abuse;
- Exploitation;
- Neglect;
- Harm;
- Harassment;
- Manipulation;
- Unsafe conduct.

---

# 5. Church Safeguarding Principles

PCN FAP is committed to:

- Treating all individuals with dignity and respect;
- Maintaining safe ministry environments;
- Promoting accountability;
- Protecting confidentiality appropriately;
- Taking safeguarding concerns seriously;
- Preventing abuse before harm occurs;
- Responding promptly to safeguarding concerns;
- Cooperating with lawful authorities where necessary;
- Ensuring responsible handling of sensitive information.

---

# 6. Types of Abuse and Misconduct

Safeguarding concerns may include but are not limited to:

- Physical abuse;
- Emotional abuse;
- Sexual abuse;
- Spiritual manipulation;
- Grooming;
- Bullying;
- Harassment;
- Exploitation;
- Neglect;
- Financial abuse;
- Online exploitation;
- Inappropriate communication with minors;
- Cyberbullying;
- Unauthorized photography or recording;
- Misuse of confidential information.

---

# 7. Digital and Online Safeguarding

PCN FAP operates online ministry and communication platforms.

To promote safe digital engagement:

- Church-controlled groups and platforms may be moderated;
- Harmful, exploitative, abusive, or inappropriate content is prohibited;
- Unauthorized contact with minors through church platforms is prohibited;
- Online counseling interactions should maintain appropriate boundaries;
- Sensitive information should not be publicly disclosed;
- Users should avoid sharing confidential information in public channels;
- Digital ministry workers may receive safeguarding guidance.

The church reserves the right to remove harmful content or restrict access where necessary.

---

# 8. Livestreams, Photography, and Media Usage

Church services and events may involve:

- Photography;
- Livestreaming;
- Video recording;
- Media publication.

Reasonable efforts may be taken to respect privacy and dignity.

Parents or guardians are encouraged to supervise minors participating in media-visible activities.

Where necessary, additional consent measures may apply for identifiable media involving children or vulnerable persons.

Users may not:

- Misuse church media;
- Manipulate church recordings deceptively;
- Re-upload sensitive content maliciously;
- Harass individuals using church media content.

---

# 9. Counseling and Prayer Ministry Safeguarding

Prayer requests, counseling discussions, testimonies, and ministry interactions may involve sensitive personal information.

Church workers handling sensitive information are expected to:

- Respect confidentiality;
- Exercise discretion;
- Avoid unnecessary disclosure;
- Protect personal information appropriately;
- Escalate safeguarding risks responsibly where safety concerns arise.

The church does not guarantee absolute confidentiality where:

- A person may be at risk of harm;
- A child may be endangered;
- Abuse allegations arise;
- Legal obligations require escalation;
- Immediate safety concerns exist.

---

# 10. Volunteer and Worker Responsibilities

All church workers and volunteers are expected to:

- Behave responsibly and respectfully;
- Maintain appropriate boundaries;
- Avoid exploitative conduct;
- Avoid private inappropriate communication with minors;
- Protect confidential information;
- Report safeguarding concerns promptly;
- Cooperate with safeguarding investigations;
- Follow church safeguarding procedures.

Failure to comply may result in disciplinary action.

---

# 11. Reporting Safeguarding Concerns

Safeguarding concerns should be reported immediately through appropriate church leadership or designated safeguarding channels.

Reports may involve:

- Abuse allegations;
- Child protection concerns;
- Harassment;
- Exploitation;
- Online misconduct;
- Threats to safety;
- Confidentiality breaches.

Reports should be handled sensitively and responsibly.

---

# 12. Safeguarding Escalation

PCN FAP reserves the right to escalate safeguarding concerns involving:

- Children;
- Vulnerable persons;
- Abuse allegations;
- Threats of violence;
- Sexual misconduct;
- Exploitation;
- Criminal activity;
- Immediate safety risks.

Escalation may involve:

- Church leadership;
- Safeguarding coordinators;
- Parents or guardians;
- Professional advisors;
- Law enforcement;
- Appropriate governmental authorities.

Escalation decisions shall consider safety, ethics, legal obligations, and pastoral responsibilities.

---

# 13. Data Protection and Confidentiality

Safeguarding-related information may include sensitive personal data.

PCN FAP shall process safeguarding information in accordance with:

- The Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023;
- Applicable legal obligations;
- Confidentiality principles;
- Church safeguarding responsibilities.

Access to safeguarding information shall be restricted to authorized individuals where reasonably necessary.

---

# 14. Retention of Safeguarding Records

Safeguarding-related records may be retained where necessary for:

- Legal compliance;
- Protection of individuals;
- Incident investigations;
- Church governance;
- Risk management;
- Safeguarding responsibilities.

Retention periods may vary depending on the nature and severity of the concern.

---

# 15. Online Safety Expectations

Users engaging with church digital platforms should:

- Interact respectfully;
- Avoid abusive conduct;
- Avoid sharing explicit or harmful content;
- Protect personal information;
- Avoid impersonation;
- Avoid exploitation or harassment;
- Respect safeguarding boundaries.

The church may restrict access for unsafe conduct.

---

# 16. Third-Party Platforms

Church ministry activities may involve third-party platforms including:

- YouTube;
- Facebook;
- Instagram;
- WhatsApp;
- Telegram;
- Zoom;
- Google services.

Users acknowledge that such platforms operate under their own policies and security practices.

PCN FAP is not responsible for third-party platform operations outside church-controlled systems.

---

# 17. Cross-Border Data Transfers

Some ministry tools, communication systems, analytics providers, cloud services, or digital platforms may process data outside Nigeria.

Where cross-border processing occurs, PCN FAP shall take reasonable steps to ensure compliance with applicable data protection obligations under the NDPA 2023.

---

# 18. Data Breach and Incident Response

In the event of a safeguarding-related security incident or personal data breach that may pose risks to individuals, PCN FAP may take reasonable response measures including:

- Internal investigation;
- Containment actions;
- Safeguarding escalation;
- Notification of relevant authorities where legally required;
- Notification of affected individuals where appropriate.

---

# 19. Non-Retaliation

Individuals who report safeguarding concerns in good faith should not face retaliation for making responsible reports.

False or malicious allegations may however result in disciplinary action where appropriate.

---

# 20. Policy Violations

Violations of this policy may result in actions including:

- Restriction of ministry activities;
- Suspension;
- Removal from volunteer responsibilities;
- Termination of access;
- Escalation to authorities where appropriate.

---

# 21. Policy Updates

PCN FAP may update this policy periodically to reflect:

- Legal requirements;
- Operational changes;
- Safeguarding improvements;
- Ministry developments;
- Technology changes.

Updated versions become effective upon publication unless otherwise stated.
